# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. Assign roles according to project size and context; one person may cover multiple roles, but each responsibility and decision should have a clear owner.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers help ensure that solutions address user needs and provide clear, accessible experiences. Depending on the project, these may be separate roles or combined.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and interaction designs
- Identify accessibility and usability needs
- Validate proposed designs with users and stakeholders
- Document design decisions and acceptance considerations

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user research and design choices to product goals and priorities.
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to make designs feasible, testable, and consistent with acceptance criteria.
- Coordinate research, reviews, and design dependencies with the Project Manager and communicate findings to relevant stakeholders.

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction and help the delivery team make sound, maintainable design decisions. The responsibilities may be shared or combined depending on project scale.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design, architecture, and implementation standards
- Surface technical risks, constraints, and dependencies early
- Support estimation and decomposition of complex work
- Facilitate technical reviews and resolve design questions
- Promote reliability, maintainability, and appropriate documentation

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers on implementation choices, reviews, and technical problem solving.
- Work with the Product Manager to explain technical trade-offs and with the Project Manager to identify dependencies, risks, and schedule implications.
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and quality strategy, and explain technical decisions to stakeholders as needed.

---

## DevOps/Site Reliability Engineers and Release Managers

### Role Summary
DevOps/Site Reliability Engineers (SREs) support delivery automation and operational reliability. Release Managers coordinate release readiness and execution. Depending on team structure, these responsibilities may be held by one role or shared across the team.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain or improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Support environment readiness, observability, and operational procedures
- Coordinate release readiness, deployment steps, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Monitor service health and help triage production issues
- Capture operational follow-up actions and reliability improvements

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers and QA/Testing to ensure builds, tests, and smoke checks support safe releases.
- Work with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and status, and with the Product Manager on launch readiness and customer impact.
- Communicate deployment status and operational concerns to stakeholders and support teams.

---

## Security and Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads advise teams on security, privacy, and compliance risks so these concerns are addressed throughout planning, implementation, and release.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Advise on threat, data-handling, and access-control risks
- Help define appropriate security checks and review findings
- Recommend mitigations and escalation for material risks
- Support incident readiness and lessons learned when relevant

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to incorporate security and privacy needs into scope and acceptance criteria.
- Advise Developers and QA/Testing on secure implementation, test coverage, and scanning findings.
- Coordinate risk tracking and escalation with the Project Manager and engage stakeholders or security responders when needed.

---

## Customer Support and Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives bring customer-facing and service-operational perspectives into delivery and help prepare teams to support changes after release.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer feedback, support trends, and operational needs
- Identify impacts to support workflows, documentation, and service readiness
- Help prepare support materials, FAQs, and escalation guidance
- Communicate customer-impacting issues and coordinate feedback after launch
- Contribute operational insights to incident reviews and retrospectives

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to relay customer needs and assess product impact.
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on readiness, stakeholder communications, and dependencies.
- Work with Developers, QA/Testing, and DevOps/SRE or Release Managers on supportability, runbooks, known issues, and post-release feedback.
